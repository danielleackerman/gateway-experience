The Gateway Experience · Wave 6

Odyssey

Six sequential Hemi-Sync® exercises that move beyond Focus 15 into increasingly nonphysical exploration. Odyssey develops sensing, expansion, movement beyond ordinary physical orientation, contact, and introduction to Focus 21. Press play on an exercise to load it.

SourceMonroe Institute · Hemi-Sync®
Format6 exercises
Wave6 — Odyssey
Outline rev1.2

01▶ play — Sensing

  • COREDevelops sensitivity to impressions and forms of information perceived beyond ordinary physical sensation.

02▶ play — Expansion

  • COREExtends awareness outward, exploring a broader sense of self and environment beyond familiar physical boundaries.

03▶ play — Point of Departure

  • COREEstablishes a deliberate point from which awareness can move beyond its ordinary physical orientation into further exploration.

04▶ play — Non-Physical Friends

  • CORECreates an opportunity for awareness of helpful nonphysical presences, contacts, or impressions encountered during exploration.

05▶ play — Introduction to Focus 21

  • COREIntroduces Focus 21, a state associated with the boundary between physical reality and increasingly nonphysical states of awareness.

06▶ play — Free Flow Journey in Focus 21

  • COREProvides an open journey in Focus 21 for self-directed exploration using the skills developed throughout Odyssey.
