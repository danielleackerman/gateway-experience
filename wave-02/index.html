Threshold

Six sequential Hemi-Sync® exercises that build on Focus 10 and introduce Focus 12 — a state of expanded awareness. Threshold develops practical tools for problem solving, patterning, energy work, and body awareness. Press play on an exercise to load it.

SourceMonroe Institute · Hemi-Sync®

03▶ play — One-Month Patterning
